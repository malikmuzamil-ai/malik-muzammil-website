The diagnosis

Why your LinkedIn is not working yet.

Referral roulette

Between 55 and 65% of the business arrives because somebody remembered you. That is a coincidence with good conversion. It is not a pipeline, and effort does not move it.

Feast and famine

Three engagements land in one month and nothing lands for two. The revenue is real. The rhythm is not, so every quarter opens with the same question about where the next one comes from.

Marketing burnout

Three posts, then three weeks of silence. Maybe an agency that produced posts and no clients. Each attempt deepens the belief that LinkedIn simply does not work for someone like you.

No client system

A profile that reads like a CV. An empty Featured section. Nobody owns marketing, because at eight to twenty clients there is no team and no hour left to become one.

The one problem underneath all four is invisible expertise. The gap between how good you are and how many of the right people know it.

The six we hear most

Fair questions.

I tried posting and nothing happened.

That tracks. Posting on its own almost never works. It is one of six moving parts, and the other five are positioning, a profile that converts, a magnet, engagement and actual conversations. You tested one and got the result that one part produces.

I do not have the time.

You would not. That is the point of us. Your total workload is one call a month and a yes or no on the content before it goes out. If it ever costs you more than that, we have built it wrong.

Will it actually sound like me?

That is the part most people get wrong, so it is fair to ask. The easiest answer is that we show you. We put together the audit on your profile first, and you see the tone before you decide anything.

I paid an agency before and got posts, not clients.

Then you already know the question to ask, which is what number we get judged on. Ours is conversations and calls booked. We do not put impressions on a client report, because you cannot do anything with them.

My clients are not on LinkedIn.

Your clients might not be. Your buyers are. HR directors, VPs, CPOs, founders. Which of those signs off on your programs?

Isn't this just ghostwriting?

Ghostwriting is posts. There are people doing that for a few hundred a month and it is a fair price for what it is. We are the whole channel: positioning, profile, magnets, engagement and the conversations. Different job, different number.
